Privacy Policy - UniversalPrint

Last updated: September 16, 2026

UniversalPrint ("the App") is an application designed to allow users to send documents and images to compatible printers through supported network connections.

1. Information We Collect

UniversalPrint does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, address, phone number, or email address.

The App may process technical information required to establish communication between the user's device, computer, and printer.

2. Files and Documents

When the user selects a document or image for printing, the file is processed only for the purpose of carrying out the requested printing operation.

UniversalPrint does not intentionally upload, sell, or use the user's documents or images for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

3. Local Network Access

The App may use the local network to communicate with compatible computers and printers.

Network addresses and connection information may be processed temporarily to establish and maintain the printing connection.

4. Internet Access

Some features may require network or Internet access depending on the printing configuration and services used by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

UniversalPrint does not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

If third-party services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

UniversalPrint does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention

UniversalPrint does not maintain a personal user database. Temporary technical information used for a printing operation is not intentionally retained longer than necessary for the requested operation.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
